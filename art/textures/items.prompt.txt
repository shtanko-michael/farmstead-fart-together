A pixel-art sprite sheet of six Minecraft item textures, 1536x1024 landscape.

Layout: a 3 x 2 grid of equal cells on ONE FLAT SOLID MAGENTA BACKGROUND (#FF00FF) everywhere: no gradient, no glow, no vignette, no shadow, no texture, no grid lines, no frames. One sprite centred in each cell, about 60% of the cell, with clean magenta all around it. No text, no numbers, no labels.

Every sprite is a real 16x16 Minecraft texture drawn big: 16 x 16 large square pixels, each pixel exactly one flat colour, hard edges, no anti-aliasing, no gradients, no blur. A 1-pixel darker outline around each shape, 3-4 shades per material, light from the top left, seen from the front like vanilla potato, beetroot, wheat seeds and mushroom stew icons.

Cells, left to right, top row then bottom row:
1. Beans: a small heap of five or six plump kidney-shaped brown-red beans, each with a light highlight pixel.
2. Cabbage seeds: four or five small pale yellow-green seeds scattered like vanilla wheat seeds.
3. Cabbage: one round green head of cabbage with curled outer leaves and lighter veins, darker green base.
4. Bowl of beans: a vanilla-style brown wooden bowl (like the mushroom stew bowl) heaped with brown-red beans in sauce.
5. Gas meter icon, only 9 x 9 large pixels (same pixel size as the others, so a smaller sprite): a small round puffy lime-green gas cloud, readable like the Minecraft heart and food HUD icons, dark outline.
6. Full gas meter icon, 9 x 9 large pixels: the same cloud shape in shining gold and yellow, with one white sparkle pixel.
