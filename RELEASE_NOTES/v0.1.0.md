First release of **Fart Together** by Farmstead: cartoon toots with real gameplay, family-friendly by default.

- Press G to toot: a pose, a soft puff of gas behind you and one of eight sounds, the same for everyone around. **Hold the key to charge** a bigger toot; a tap is a small one. The gas meter shows the build-up.
- Gas comes from food: the mod's own beans and cabbage (crops, chest loot, farmer trades), a bowl of beans, and vanilla foods. An enchanted golden apple makes the next toot golden; milk settles everything.
- Gas clouds linger and scare mobs away (harmless unless the server adds effects).
- **Jet jump**: toot in mid-air to fly a little higher and land softly. Under water a toot is a stream of bubbles.
- **Fart Together**: toot at the same moment as friends nearby for one big shared cloud, a chorus and a combo counter.
- Nine advancements, your own style (colour, particles, sound, pitch, pose) that friends see, a "My style" tab with a live preview, streamer-safe mode.
- Server rules with presets, `/fart` commands, LuckPerms nodes and meta. 12 languages.
- Minecraft 1.20.1 (Fabric, Forge); 1.21–1.21.5, 1.21.7–1.21.11, 26.1–26.3 (Fabric, NeoForge). **Fabric needs Fabric API**; NeoForge and Forge need nothing. Install on the server and on clients.
